# 22 — Women-led apps: what a women's calorie tracker should borrow

Research pass (2026-06-10) on Waves Women + women-led / cycle-aware / life-stage apps, filtered through HerFuel's objective (**a calorie/macro tracker for women** — not a period tracker; honest, anti-shame, privacy-first).

## Per-app takeaways
- **Waves Women** (waveswomen.com) — AI "hormonal health companion" for peri-aged women ("is it my hormones?"). No nutrition/calories. **Borrow:** the **"Experiments" mechanic** (small, science-backed, opt-in habit trials you test for days/weeks then review) and the **anti-to-do-list** framing.
- **Wild.AI** — cites "450+ papers"; supports natural cycle / **birth-control types** / peri/menopause; wearable sync. **Borrow:** evidence-transparency posture; a **hormonal-contraception branch** (HC users have no natural phases — honesty). *Caution: it pushes phase nutrient "demands" — fine as food suggestions, not target changes.*
- **FitrWoman** (Orreco) — **recipe/food library filterable by phase/symptom/nutrient/energy/recovery/sleep** + credible resource library. **Borrow:** the filtering UX (content, safe).
- **MyFLO / Flo Living** — "cycle-syncing diet." **Skip the prescriptive four-phase eating** (under-evidenced; first validation study only just launched). Borrow only engaging phase-aware content delivery.
- **28 (28.co)** — explicit "**no more calorie counting**" positioning. Tells us the market objection to answer: "honest numbers without the obsession."
- **Caria** (perimenopause) — evidence-based toolkit; **nutritionist recipes that cite research**; symptom score → individualized tips. **Borrow:** name the expert, cite the source; symptom log that *contextualizes* (not changes) the target.
- **Perelel** — best-in-class **life-stage segmentation** (conception / each trimester / postpartum / menopause) each with a per-stage *why* + third-party-tested trust badges. **Borrow:** the stage model + per-stage rationale copy.
- **Stardust** — "your data is private, period." **Borrow the posture**; **cautionary tale**: TechCrunch found it sharing data + walking back E2E claims → *only claim privacy you can prove.*
- **PCOS apps** (Nutrola etc.) — evidence-backed **macro distribution** shift for insulin resistance: higher protein (~30%, 80–120 g), fibre 25–30 g, lower-GI carbs. **Borrow** for the PCOS branch (adjust *distribution*, not a calorie penalty). Reviews say most PCOS apps "lack credible content" → credibility is the wedge.

## Synthesis — adopt / adapt / skip
**ADOPT (fits a calorie/macro tracker):** life-stage target engine with transparent rationale (pregnancy +0/+340/+450 by trimester; lactation +330–500 — solid science) · **PCOS macro mode** (distribution shift, cited) · cited expert-attributed nutrition content + filterable recipe library · evidence-transparency everywhere.
**ADAPT LIGHTLY:** Waves "**Experiments**" (opt-in habit trials w/ review — anti-shame vs streaks) · symptom/cycle log that *contextualizes* not moves the target · wearable sync to inform expenditure (skip "readiness score") · birth-control branch.
**SKIP:** **cycle-phase calorie bumps** (luteal RMR rise is small + not significant post-2000, ES 0.23 p=0.055; 1.7–10% individual — auto-bumping violates "only move it when science supports it"; at most an optional flagged "honor your hunger" note) · cycle-syncing diet · lunar/cosmic framing · fertility diagnostics / telehealth / full period prediction (out of scope).
**TRUST/SAFETY (P0):** ED-safe design (MFP "contributed to my disorder" 73% / 30% "very much" — the *number-obsession* is the mechanism): optional hide-the-number, no aggressive streak pressure, sensible **minimum-calorie floor**, **never restrict during pregnancy/lactation**, ED screening + signposting · provable privacy (woman-led, never-sell — only if real).

**Sources:** Waves, Wild.AI, FitrWoman, MyFLO, 28, Caria, Perelel, Stardust/TechCrunch; RMR meta PMC7357764; pregnancy/lactation kcal PMC5104202 / NICHD; PCOS RCT PMC10381787; ED risk PMC5700836.
