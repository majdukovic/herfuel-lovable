# Name & ASO — recommendation

**Date:** 2026-06-09 · **Status:** ✅ **DECIDED — HerFuel** (working name; applied across live docs + mockup-v3) · full clearance still pending
**Brief:** a name for the reframed product (women's life-stage nutrition, perinatal-led) that (a) aids **App Store discovery (ASO)**, (b) is **recognizable** — a woman roughly gets what it is, (c) **spans the journey** (not stage-locked to "cycle" *or* "mama"), (d) is **clearable**.

> ⚠️ **Reality check from research:** the women's-health/nutrition name space is **brutally saturated.** I checked ~15 candidates; nearly all collide *in-category*: **Nuri, Nura, Nara, Nouri, Nourish, Mela, Juna, Juno, Juniper, Vella, Plenna, Matria, Lumara, Perla** are all taken by health/nutrition/women's brands. Pretty one-word femtech names (Flo, Frida, Oula, Maven, Mira, Bloom, Foria, Willow, Ovia) are gone. **This pushes us toward a distinctive/compound name + a keyword subtitle.**

## The ASO insight that matters more than the word
On the App Store, **discovery comes from the name *field* + subtitle + keyword field, not the brand alone.** So the brand can be distinctive while the subtitle carries the high-volume search terms. Terms women actually search (verified): *pregnancy nutrition tracker · postpartum nutrition · breastfeeding macro tracker · women's nutrition · macro tracker · folate/iron/choline.*

**Recommended store identity:**
- **App name (≤30 chars):** `HerFuel: Women's Nutrition`
- **Subtitle (≤30 chars):** `Pregnancy, postpartum & cycle`
- **Keyword field (100 chars):** `pregnancy,postpartum,breastfeeding,nutrition,macro,calorie,women,folate,iron,cycle,pcos,mom,prenatal`

## Recommendation: **HerFuel** (primary)
Why it wins on *this* brief:
- **Recognizable at a glance** — "Her" + "Fuel" = women's nutrition/energy. Meets the "knows what it is" goal better than any coined word.
- **Spans the journey** — not locked to cycle or motherhood; works pregnancy → perimenopause.
- **Natural evolution of "CycleFuel"** — drop *Cycle*, keep the **Fuel** equity, broaden *Cycle → Her* (all of her, every stage). Clean migration narrative.
- **Appears clearable** — no app collision surfaced (vs the saturated field); "Fuel Her Hustle"/"Fuelin" exist but are distinct.
- Pairs perfectly with the ASO subtitle above.

**Trade-off (be honest):** "fuel" is common in fitness/nutrition → **moderate trademark strength** and slightly performance/gym-coded vs our warm, anti-shame tone. Mitigate with warm visual branding + the gentle in-app voice.

## Backups (ranked) — if you want something more premium/ownable
1. **Maela** — warm, feminine, journey-neutral, brandable (femtech convention). Only collision found: an IG dietician + "Mavella" superfood (minor). *Needs clearance.* Weaker instant-meaning → leans hard on the subtitle.
2. **Coined "vita/nourish-adjacent" word** (e.g., *Vitala*, *Nourela*) — signals nutrition via root, but the "nour/nutri/vita" space is collision-heavy → **verify first.**
3. **Keep distinctive + descriptive combos** — e.g., *EveryStage*, *Stages Nutrition* — very clear + ASO-friendly, but generic/weak TM.

> ❌ Avoid: anything in **Nour/Nuri/Nara/Nura/Mela/Juna/Juno** (taken), and **any "Mama/Mom/Preg/Cycle"** lock (re-narrows the journey — the exact mistake we just fixed, in reverse).

## First-pass clearance — done 2026-06-09 (initial signals)
- ✅ **No "HerFuel" app** found on the App Store / Google Play.
- ⚠️ **"HERFUEL" women's pre-workout supplement** exists (HerSupps, hersuppsnutrition.com) — same name, **adjacent category** (women's nutrition products). Real trademark consideration: different goods class than a tracking app, but same space → potential confusion. **Assess with counsel before launch.**
- ⚠️ **herfuel.com is taken** (resolves). `herfuel.app` / `tryherfuel.com` appear unregistered (verify at a registrar) — plan a domain variant.
- Similar-but-distinct: "Fuel Her Hustle," "Fuelin," "FitFuel" (no exact-name app collision).

## Competitor naming landscape (context for differentiation)
The perinatal-nutrition shelf is filling: **LatchFit** (breastfeeding macro tracker), **PregnancyPlate**, **Fittur** (pregnancy food), **Mommie**, **mamamacros**, **Loam**, **Nutrola**. Note they're nearly all **single-stage** and **descriptive/mom-locked**. Our name should signal the **whole journey** (the differentiator) — another reason "HerFuel" (journey-neutral) beats a "mama"-locked name.
