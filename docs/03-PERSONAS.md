# Personas v0.2 — reframed (perinatal-led, full-journey)

**Date:** 2026-06-09 · **Status:** Draft for iteration (validate in interviews) · **Supersedes** the cycle-first v0.1 framing.

> Reframed per `14-POSITIONING-REASSESSMENT.md`: the **primary MVP target is now a perinatal woman** (the loudest demand + strongest evidence + highest pain). The cycle-aware woman becomes a *journey stage* persona (acquisition-secondary, retention-critical). Build for **Persona 1 first**. All personas are hypotheses to confirm with 8–12 interviews.

> **The journey:** these personas are the same woman at different life-stages. The product follows her across them — which is the retention thesis.

---

## Persona 1 — "Overwhelmed New Mom" — Sofia, 31 🎯 PRIMARY (acquisition wedge)
**Snapshot:** 9 weeks postpartum, breastfeeding, first baby, iPhone, time-poor and exhausted.
**Current tools:** A baby-feeding tracker (tracks the *baby*, not her) + occasionally re-opens MyFitnessPal.
**Goals:** Have energy, protect her milk supply, feel like herself again — *without* crash-dieting.
**Frustrations:**
- MFP tells her she's "almost overweight" and sets a deficit — **ignoring that breastfeeding burns ~400–500 kcal/day.** It shames a body that's doing something extraordinary.
- No app accounts for breastfeeding's real, higher needs; she's scared of hurting her supply.
- She has 30 seconds, one free hand, and decision fatigue — logging has to be effortless.
**What wins her:** An **honest, higher target** ("+400 kcal today to make milk — real science, not a cheat"), supply-safe guidance (no deficit before ~6–8 weeks), one-handed fast logging, zero shame.
**Quote (hypothesis):** *"Every app acts like I'm just trying to lose the baby weight. I'm trying to keep a human alive and not fall apart."*
**Why she matters:** Loudest unmet demand in the market (`12`), strongest/established evidence, highest emotional pain, biggest gap (apps track the baby, not the mother). She's the wedge.

---

## Persona 2 — "Nourishing Two" — Amara, 33 🎯 PRIMARY (perinatal, pregnancy)
**Snapshot:** 22 weeks pregnant, second trimester, health-motivated, anxious about doing it right.
**Current tools:** A pregnancy *week-by-week* app (baby size, not her nutrition) + Google.
**Goals:** Nourish her baby and herself; hit folate/iron/protein; gain weight in a healthy range without obsessing.
**Frustrations:**
- Pregnancy apps treat nutrition as an afterthought; general trackers don't know she's pregnant (or scare her with "overweight").
- Conflicting "what's safe to eat" noise; doesn't know her real calorie/nutrient needs by trimester.
**What wins her:** Trimester-aware targets (+340 kcal T2, established), the nutrients that matter (folate/iron/choline) surfaced, food-safety awareness, no shame about healthy weight gain.
**Quote (hypothesis):** *"I just want to know I'm giving the baby what it needs — and that I'm okay too."*
**Why she matters:** High motivation + willingness to pay; the entry point *before* Sofia (the journey: pregnancy → postpartum). Same woman, next stage.

---

## Persona 3 — "Cycle-Aware Switcher" — Maya, 29 (journey stage; retention-critical)
**Snapshot:** Naturally cycling, fitness-engaged, between life-stages (pre-pregnancy or postpartum-returned).
**Pain & wins (unchanged from v0.1):** juggles MFP + Flo; blindsided by luteal cravings; wants fast logging + cycle empathy + the honest "your target stays steady, we adjust the plan" message (we don't fake the luteal bump). Iron-for-fatigue flag lands here too.
**Why she matters:** The **persistent stage** that retains women between/after perinatal phases — the connective tissue of the journey. Strong acquisition channel in her own right (largest population), but no longer the *lead* wedge.

---

## Persona 4 — "PCOS Fighter" — Priya, 30 (cross-cutting mode; high WTP)
**Snapshot:** PCOS diagnosis, insulin-resistant, frustrated by years of generic advice.
**Goals:** Manage symptoms, steady energy/cravings, lose weight kindly.
**Wins:** Lower-GI / insulin-aware coaching (well-studied), symptom tracking, supportive non-shame tone. Proven willingness-to-pay ($17–28/mo, Cysterhood).
**Why she matters:** Persistent retention + high intent; a cross-cutting mode (any life-stage). Strong P1 expansion.

---

## Persona 5 — "Recovering Calorie-Counter" — Sam, 26 (cross-cutting; protect + wedge)
**Snapshot:** History of anxious calorie-counting; wants structure without spiraling.
**Wins:** **No-numbers mode**, nourishment framing, zero shame. *Especially* important in perinatal (postpartum bodies + DE risk overlap).
**Why she matters:** Validates anti-shame as a differentiator and is the persona our safety design must protect — now higher-stakes given perinatal.

*(Later-stage personas: "Perimenopause" — protein/muscle, persistent, P2; "Quantified Optimizer" — Oura/micros depth, P1/P2 magnet. Don't let either bloat the MVP.)*

---

## How the personas shape priority
- **Build the MVP for Sofia (Persona 1)** — honest breastfeeding targets + effortless one-handed logging + zero shame.
- **Amara (Persona 2)** shares the same engine (the entry stage before Sofia) — perinatal is the wedge.
- **Maya (Persona 3)** is the retention stage — supported, not the headline.
- **Priya (4)** and **Sam (5)** are served by the cross-cutting modes (PCOS) + safety/no-numbers design.
