HerFuel v5

Comprehensive prototype: a great general macro tracker + optional life-stage modules + device integrations — with a built-in feedback mode for interviews.

For participants — try this
  1. It opens as a plain, great tracker. Top has a swipeable summary — swipe / tap the dots between Calories & macros → Move & hydrate → nutrients that matter for you (iron, calcium, fibre — adapts to your module). Food is grouped by Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner (add a Snack section in Me).
  2. Change the day from the strip at the very top (Cal AI–style) — tap a past day to see those logs, or a future day to plan.
  3. Today’s tips are expanded when a module is on (they’ve earned the space); the general-mode promo starts collapsed — tap any header to toggle. Water & steps live on the Move & hydrate carousel page (swipe to the 3rd dot to log water).
  4. Tips adapt, they’re not static. Turn on a module → drag the week / cycle-day scrubber on the target card (pregnancy target moves T1→T2→T3) and watch the focus + tips change. Expand “Today’s tips”, tap “How do you feel today?” → log Nausea → a matching tip appears with a “Because…” line. Tips also react to nutrient gaps in what you’ve logged.
  5. Progress → Milestones (Cal AI-style): Day-streak & Badges-earned tiles + a hexagon badge grid (tap a badge for the unlock detail; View all badges). Optional (toggle in Me); counts check-ins, never deficits; a 0 streak shows a fresh start, not a loss.
  6. Tap ＋ → it fans open in place (the screen stays) with Log exercise · Search food database · Scan food · Saved foods & meals (+ More for voice / barcode / create / copy). Only after you pick one does a screen open → AI draft → confirm into a meal section. Tap ＋ (now ×) or the dimmed area to collapse.
  7. Meals tab — recipe & meal ideas, meal plans & saved meals, tailored to your active life-stage (like Carb Manager's Meals, phase-aware).
  8. Me → Life-stage modules → turn on Breastfeeding (or Pregnancy/Cycle/PCOS/Perimenopause) → Today's honest target, coaching, Meals & articles all adapt. Turn off → clean tracker.
  9. Me → Connected apps → Connect Oura/Whoop/Apple Health → live data fills Progress, incl. a Cycle & body category (cycle length, flow, BBT/skin-temp). Or tap ＋ Log today's symptoms to add manually. Disconnect → gone.
💬 Feedback mode

Turn it on (button above, or the in-app bar) → tap any feature to leave a comment; "General" for overall thoughts; Export → Send to me (auto-collects to the founder's inbox once a Formspree URL is set in app.js) or Copy. For richer multi-user analytics, also use Maze — see README.
